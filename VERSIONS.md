  drawing (they were mocked without a key); controller vs entity must be explicit; unlocks by drawing are key;

- **Top problems, by impact:**
  1. **(high) Add-a-button never works with real generation, so a round cannot be finished.**
     - A clear photo of DRILL or LAND came back "unreadable button" 4/4 times, and a finger-drawn LAND came back
       "not JSON".
     - The same image re-sent with the region set to the whole image was read as LAND, so the prompt is the cause: it
       says the image is the whole pad and asks the model to read one region, but the phone sends a tight crop.
     - DRILL and DIG unlock by themselves at the 3:00 assists, LAND never does.
     - Evidence: `firsttimer-23..25`, `-52..55`, `diag-01..03`.
     - Fix: the prompt fix and bigger output budgets in e2f3641, plus a 3-call live-generation smoke in every version
       gate (the mock hid this).
  2. **(high) You die while drawing, nothing tells you, and you restart 276 m back.**
     - The add sheet leaves the ship flying under boss fire, and a death shows only a small "RESPAWN 3…" label.
     - Evidence: `firsttimer-15`, `-24`, `griefer-13`.
     - Fix: protect the ship while a sheet is open; show a big "DESTROYED by X · back in 3" card; respawn near the
       objective you reached.
  3. **(high) PvP is spawn camping.**
     - Mallory killed Bob 20 times in about 3 minutes without dying (4030 vs −1000 points).
     - Bob had no name tag (tags go to the first 8 joiners, all bots), so Mallory could not tell him from the bots.
     - Bob got no warning and no hit direction, and the TV camera never showed the duel.
     - Evidence: `griefer-10`, `-11`, `-13`, `-14`.
     - Fix: spread the spawns; a spawn shield that lasts until you move or fire; a score floor; tag humans first; a
       hit-direction marker; a TV director that cuts to human fights.
  4. **(high) The headline promise is missing.** There is no "draw your ship" step, and every ship and explorer is the
     default model. Evidence: `firsttimer-02`, `-03`, any play shot. Fix: "1 · DRAW YOUR SHIP" first, serve
     `inflate.js`, and build the ship from the drawing.
  5. **(medium) Instructions.**
     - The tools are unlabelled, and the riddle vanishes after 4 s.
     - The HUD and the TV spell out the answer ("HOLD DRILL CLOSE", "MAYA Draw LAND").
     - Error messages are raw internal strings.
     - Evidence: `firsttimer-07`, `-15`, `-50`, `diag-02`.
     - Fix: labelled tools, the riddle pinned under the objective, plain-language errors, hints only on the player's
       own phone.
  6. **(medium) The phone radar is upside down:** the boss dead ahead is drawn below your arrow. Evidence:
     `firsttimer-13`. Fix: one sign at `controller.html:955`.
  7. **(medium) Bright scenes wash out the HUD:** the pink nebula, the planet glow, and a white-out at the boss kill on
     both screens. Evidence: `firsttimer-49`, `-50`, `video-frames-regular-1.jpg` (0:40). Fix: outlined text, scrims
     behind the HUD, a bloom clamp on flashes.
  8. **(medium) The TV is only half readable from the sofa.**
     - JOIN, the URL and the countdown read; scores, race time and status (10-14 px) do not.
     - The QR is about 15 cm wide on a 55" TV, so you have to stand within about 1.5 m.
     - 25 result rows overflow the screen, and the win banner crosses them.
     - Evidence: `host-across-room-lobby-25p.png`, `host-across-room-results-25p.png`, `firsttimer-08`.
     - Fix: 28-40 px TV type, a bigger QR, a results card with the top 3 large, no name tags in the lobby or results.
  9. **(low) Not Fortnite yet.** Fix: Bebas Neue or Barlow Condensed italic headings, chunky slanted panels, white
     text with a dark outline.
- **Highlights:**
  - A photo of a paper controller becomes working phone controls in 2-3 s, placed where they were drawn (2/2).
  - The TV lobby (big JOIN, QR, three steps) and the boss fight are the most watchable moments.
  - 0 console errors in every run.
- **Confirmed findings** (each with file:line and fix in `.orch/runs/v1.0-findings.md`): the 34 blocker and major
  reports collapse to 22 issues.
  - **19 confirmed** (3 blockers, 16 majors), 3 downgraded to minor, 0 disproved.
  - **Blockers:**
    1. Add-a-button reads the wrong region (promoted from major on the live 0/5).
    2. The output-token caps are shared with reasoning and no reasoning effort is set.
    3. `GET //` crashes the server.
  - **Majors:**
    - Generation: a controller timeout silently gives the default layout; a wrong-kind drawing is accepted; a layout
      with no steering is accepted; icon-only controls are misread.
    - Photos: coloured pens are not detected; anything touching the frame edge is deleted; rotation wipes the add sheet.
    - Rendering and TV: the drawn ship and explorer are never rendered; the radar is mirrored; the TV names two
      winners; the boss model is 1.6× its hit radius; the rock field is rebuilt on every kill; name tags go only to the
      first 8 or 12 joiners; an explorer can spawn stuck (about 1.8% of 25-player rounds).
    - Server: any POST creates a ghost player; no name-to-device binding.
- **Fixed in later versions** (not yet verified live):
  - Commit e2f3641 (in v1.1): the add-a-button prompt now treats the image as the cropped control alone; output budgets
    are 1200 tokens per button and 2000 per controller, with a default reasoning effort; wrong-kind drawings are refused
    (`looksLike`).
  - v1.1 also serves `inflate.js`, switches to "most points wins" (re-check the win banner), and enlarges the boss
    hit radius to 20.
  - Still open at HEAD 5238fcf (01:05): the `GET //` crash (`server.js:255`), the mirrored radar
    (`controller.html:955`), name tags for the first N joiners, the explorer spawn check, ghost players via `/input`, PvP
